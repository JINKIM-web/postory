## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
The design system embodies the ethos of a master editorial publishing atelier merged with intelligent, quiet technology. It rejects the hyper-saturated, gradient-heavy visual tropes of consumer AI, opting instead for restraint, clarity, and institutional trust. It is designed for creators, editors, and brands who view storytelling as high-value craftsmanship. The experience feels like stepping into a serene, sunlit Scandinavian reading room: warm paper surfaces, rich charcoal ink typography, and purposeful terracotta accents.

### Visual Aesthetic & Movement
The design movement is **Warm Editorial Modernism**—combining the clean structural logic of Swiss typography with warm tactile paper tones and subtle physical affordances. 

Key attributes include:
- **Calm, High-Trust Surfaces**: Pure white workspace panels floating over warm tinted backgrounds (`#FAFAF7`), creating a natural separation of focus without visual noise.
- **Evidence-Driven Clarity**: Visual design prioritizes factual grounding and semantic status over cosmetic decoration. Color is functional, reserved strictly for state changes, verified data, and primary actions.
- **Content-First Canvas**: The UI recedes into the background, providing generous typographic breathing room, high line-heights for comfortable long-form editing, and crisp ink contrast.
- **Anti-Patterns**: Strictly avoid neon glows, cybernetic dark themes, glassy drop shadows, novelty micro-interactions, and chat-bubble metaphors.

## Colors

The color palette centers on warm paper tones, deep carbon inks, and an energetic terracotta-coral accent that directs focus without inducing visual fatigue.

### Color Roles & Semantics
- **Primary (`#F2664B`)**: The terracotta-coral accent. Applied strictly to forward momentum: primary calls-to-action ("스토리 만들기"), active tab underlines, progressive stage highlights, and selected distribution channels.
- **Secondary (`#171717`)**: Dense charcoal ink used for foundational UI architecture, primary structural headers, navigational markers, and high-emphasis labels.
- **Tertiary (`#15803D`)**: Forest green for factual verification, confirmed evidence markers, and resolved editorial checks.
- **Neutral (`#737373`)**: Balanced neutral for structural borders, secondary metadata, inactive controls, and contextual helper microcopy.

### Semantic Status System
All status indicators operate with a dedicated two-tone system (rich readable foreground on high-tint muted background):
- **Verified / Factual (`#15803D` / `#F0FDF4`)**: Confirmed statements extracted directly from verified data sources.
- **Cautionary / Unverified (`#B45309` / `#FFFBEB`)**: Content requiring human review or missing supporting evidence.
- **Alert / Policy Violation (`#DC2626` / `#FEF2F2`)**: Severe checks, prohibited terms, or hyperbole risks.
- **Visual Evidence (`#1D4ED8` / `#EFF6FF`)**: Direct observations derived from image analysis.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy prioritizes legibility, editorial cadence, and sustained focus across multi-channel drafting.

### Font Family & Texture
- **Primary Typeface**: Plus Jakarta Sans is selected for its clean, geometric balance paired with warm, humanist apertures. It mirrors the crisp legibility of contemporary Korean/Latin editorial systems like Pretendard.
- **Monospace Companion**: JetBrains Mono serves for inline tokens, metadata flags, asset names (`01_매장외관.jpg`), and numeric index references.

### Typographic Principles
- **Editorial Leading**: The long-form drafting area (`body-editorial`) enforces a generous line-height of `1.75` (28px on 16px font), preserving visual comfort during intensive proofreading and editing.
- **UI Density**: Utility and dashboard surfaces (`body-ui`) employ a tighter `1.45` ratio (22px on 15px font) for high-information density inside inspection sidebars, form parameters, and card grids.
- **Negative Letter Spacing**: Large display heads utilize subtle negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.025em`) to tighten optical word shapes, conveying editorial authority.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure
The application follows a structured 3-tier container model designed around focused production:
1. **Global App Frame**: Persistent left navigational rail (`230px` width) housing core workspaces, autosave status, and user storage metrics.
2. **Main Workspace Window**: Centered viewport capped at a maximum width of `1360px`.
3. **Editor Column Isolation**: The rich-text drafting canvas is constrained to an optimal reading column of `780px` centered within the primary stage, preventing excessive line lengths and eye travel.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 3-pane layout active. Left navigation (`230px`), primary document column (`780px`), and right context inspection drawer (`320px–360px`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: Left navigation collapses into a condensed icon rail (`64px`). The right context drawer collapses into an on-demand modal overlay accessible via quick-action tab chips.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: Single-column linear layout. Navigation moves to a compact top bar. Editor functions stack vertically. The context drawer transitions into an edge-anchored bottom sheet. Primary actions fix to a sticky bottom dock (`44px` minimum tap height).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering** and **Ambient Micro-Shadows** rather than exaggerated drop shadows or blur-heavy glassmorphism.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Level (Ground)**: `#FAFAF7` / `#F7F7F3`. The baseline background for the application shell and side rails.
2. **Card & Surface Level (Elevation 0)**: `#FFFFFF` with a hairline warm outline (`1px solid #EAEAE5`). This provides soft, crisp separation without heavy shadows.
3. **Floating Context Level (Elevation 1)**: Modals, active context toolbars, floating AI refactoring menus (`다시 쓰기`, `더 짧게`), and popovers. Elevated with an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.03)`.
4. **Overlay Level (Elevation 2)**: Full preview dialogs, image inspection modals, and platform sync drawers: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.1)`. Supported by a warm muted backdrop: `rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The design language uses a balanced continuous-curve radius (`10px` to `14px`) that communicates approachability while maintaining a clean, structured frame.

### Radius System
- **Core Containers (Cards, Panels, Canvas Boxes, Modals)**: Standardized at `0.75rem` (12px), bridging the gap between soft comfort and clean editorial alignment.
- **Interactive Pills (Status Badges, Platform Selectors, Step Chips)**: Fully rounded `9999px` to create distinct interactive cues against rectangular content surfaces.
- **Action Buttons**: Unified at `0.625rem` (10px) to echo the curvature of card frames and prevent visual tension when aligned within toolbars.
- **Inline Image Markers**: Structured blocks inside the editorial canvas utilize `0.5rem` (8px) corners to preserve the feeling of integrated document architecture.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#F2664B`, text `#FFFFFF`, radius `10px`, font weight `600`. Hover state deepens to `#E05338`. Active state scales subtly (`transform: scale(0.99)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #EAEAE5`, text `#171717`. Hover state triggers background `#F7F7F3` with border `#E5E5E0`.
- **Destructive**: Background `#FEF2F2`, border `1px solid #FECACA`, text `#DC2626`. Hover state deepens to `#DC2626` text with `#FEE2E2` surface.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chip**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `4px 12px`, font size `12px`, weight `500`. Combines a 6px semantic color dot, text label, and subtle tinted background:
  - *Draft*: Gray tint (`#F7F7F3`, text `#737373`).
  - *Generated*: Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`, text `#B45309`).
  - *Reviewed / Confirmed*: Muted green tint (`#F0FDF4`, text `#15803D`).
- **Platform Selector Chips**: Pill-shaped with brand icon + channel title (`Naver Blog`, `Instagram`, `Shorts`). Inactive uses `#FFFFFF` with `#EAEAE5` border. Selected state applies `#FFF1EE` surface with `#F2664B` border and ink.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with `1px solid #EAEAE5` outline, `10px` radius, padding `10px 14px`. Focus state applies a crisp `1px solid #F2664B` border with a subtle `0 0 0 3px rgba(242, 102, 75, 0.12)` focus ring.
- **Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)**: Rounded `4px` (checkbox) or circular (radio), `#FFFFFF` surface, `#E5E5E0` border. Active state transitions to `#F2664B` fill with white checkmark.

### Cards & Grouping Elements
- **Editor Paper Canvas**: `#FFFFFF` background, `12px` radius, subtle hairline border `#EAEAE5`. Inset reading padding of `3rem` desktop and `1.25rem` mobile.
- **Evidence Card**: `#FFFFFF` surface, left-hand decorative indicator border (`3px solid`) coded by evidence class (`#15803D` for confirmed fact, `#1D4ED8` for photo extraction). Contains compact action triggers (`수정 적용`, `무시`).

### Inline Image Marker Block
- Non-text rich block embedded in the editorial flow.
- Features a warm `#F7F7F3` surface, dashed border `#E5E5E0`, inline `48px` thumbnail, sequence indicator (`[사진 1]`), and quick reposition controls.
